What the audit covers

Six areas we check

An inspection by the marshal office or the environmental inspectorate does not have to be a surprise.

Register entry

Sections, company data and the BDO number on documents.

Waste records

Waste transfer cards (KPO) and record cards (KEO).

Reports

Completeness and accuracy of reports from recent years.

Fees

Annual fee, product fee and any arrears.

Contracts

Contracts with recovery organisations and waste collectors.

Report and plan

A list of risks and concrete remedial steps with deadlines.

Who it is for

When an audit makes sense

  • Before an inspection. When you have received a notice of inspection or a request from the office.
  • After a change of the person in charge. When BDO was handled by someone who has left the company.
  • Before a sale or merger. Environmental due diligence for a buyer or investor.
  • When you are not sure. The entry was made long ago and the company has changed since.

FAQ

Questions about the BDO audit

How long does an audit take?

Usually from a few days to two weeks, depending on the number of years and the scale of the business.

What if the audit finds errors?

We prepare report corrections and an entry update. Correcting early reduces the risk of fines.

Who can inspect BDO compliance?

Most often the regional marshal office and the regional environmental protection inspectorate.
