EPR in short

What exactly is EPR?

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is an EU environmental policy principle. The company that places packaging and certain products on the market is responsible for their collection and recycling.

Depending on the country and sales model, the obligation can fall on the manufacturer, the seller or whoever first places the goods on the market. In practice it usually means registering, reporting quantities, paying fees and meeting each country’s rules separately.

    Ask a question
    What is EPR and does it apply to me?

    Extended Producer Responsibility makes the company that first places a product or its packaging on a country's market pay for its collection and recycling. If you sell to consumers in another EU country, you are usually the producer there, even when you ship from Poland.

    I sell to Germany. Do I need to register there?

    In most cases, yes. Packaging that reaches German consumers needs a LUCID registration and a contract with a dual system, and electronics need a WEEE registration. Marketplaces check these numbers and can block offers without them.

    Do I need an Authorized Representative?

    Some countries, Germany for WEEE among them, require a local Authorized Representative if you have no company there. We provide this service in Poland and in other markets.

    What does PPWR change for online sellers?

    The Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation applies from 12 August 2026, with more requirements phased in over the following years. Recyclability, packaging labelling and limits on empty space in parcels matter most for e-commerce.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the number of countries, product streams and quantities. The first analysis of your obligations is free, and after it you get a concrete quote.

    Can I start with one country?

    Yes. Many clients start with Germany or Poland and add markets as they grow. Everything stays with one contact person and in one EPRpilot account.
