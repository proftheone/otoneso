Who it is for

Who needs a BDO entry

  • New companies. The entry is needed before starting an activity that requires it.
  • Shops starting to ship goods. Shipping products in packaging means placing packaging on the market.
  • Importers. You bring goods into Poland, so you are responsible for their packaging.
  • Companies with new products. A new category, e.g. electronics or batteries, often needs a new section in the entry.

FAQ

Questions about BDO registration

How long does BDO registration take?

Preparing the application usually takes us a few days. The time to make the entry depends on the marshal office, typically from a few days to a few weeks.

How much does the entry cost?

The mandatory registration fee is PLN 100 for micro-enterprises and PLN 300 for other companies. The annual fee is the same. We quote our own fee after a free consultation.

Where should the BDO number appear?

On invoices, on documents related to the registered activity, and on your website if you sell online.

Can I operate before getting the entry?

No. Running an activity that requires an entry without a BDO number can lead to a fine of PLN 10,000 to PLN 1,000,000.
