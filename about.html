About Otoneso

The people behind Otoneso

We are a small team of EPR practitioners with backgrounds in e-commerce, environmental law and compliance. We know the registers, the deadlines and the pitfalls, because we deal with them every day.

Our mission

EU environmental obligations, turned into a process that just works

Compliance shouldn’t be a spreadsheet.

Every EU country runs its own EPR system, with its own registers, forms, fees and deadlines. For a company selling across Europe, that quickly becomes a full-time job nobody signed up for.

Our goal is to make these obligations clear, orderly and reliable. We take over registration, reporting and settlements for packaging, WEEE, batteries and single-use plastics, so our clients can focus on selling.

We combine hands-on service with EPRpilot, our own platform for tracking obligations across markets, including the new PPWR requirements.
